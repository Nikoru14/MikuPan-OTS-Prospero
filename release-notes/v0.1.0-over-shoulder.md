The first build with the modern **Over the Shoulder** camera, an alternative
to the original fixed camera angles.

## New

- **Over the Shoulder camera view.** Choose it in **Settings › Game ›
  Camera › Camera View** (Fixed, Over the Shoulder or First Person).
  - The camera follows Miku from behind and beside her. The right stick or
    the mouse orbits it, and clicking the right stick centres it behind her.
  - The left stick walks Miku wherever you push, as seen through the camera.
  - It pulls in against walls, doors and furniture so the view is never
    blocked. Thin props such as ropes are looked through.
  - Raising the Camera Obscura turns Miku towards where the camera was
    looking; lowering it blends the camera back over her shoulder.
  - The flashlight follows the camera.
  - It drifts back behind Miku while she walks and the right stick is idle.
- **Camera settings:** shoulder side, distance, height, sensitivity, field of
  view, collision and auto-follow, saved in `mikupan.ini` under
  `[over_shoulder_camera]`. Finer tuning lives in the overlay's
  **Debug › Camera** menu (F1).
- French, German, Spanish and Italian translations for the new options.

## Unchanged

Cutscenes, apparition events, save points and ghost battles keep their
original cameras.

## Known issues

- Examining some objects briefly flashes the original fixed angle for a few
  frames. Fixed in v0.1.1.

## Build

- Release packages are now Debug builds.

## Installing

1. Download the zip for your platform and your game's region: **US** for the
   NTSC-U (USA) disc, **EU** for the PAL (EU) disc. NTSC-J discs are not
   supported.
2. Extract it, then copy `IMG_HD.BIN` and `IMG_BD.BIN` from your disc next to
   the `MikuPan` executable.
3. In game, open **Settings › Game › Camera** and set **Camera View** to
   **Over the Shoulder**.

These packages are Debug builds, as the README recommends: the Release
configuration has known issues.
