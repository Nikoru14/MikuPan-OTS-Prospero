## Fixed

- **No more flash of the fixed camera when examining objects.** Examining
  something (for example the lion mask holding the Red Handmirror) runs a
  short event, and the camera used to hand control back for those few
  frames. It now stays over the shoulder through plain events and holds
  still. Scripted camera angles still take over as before.
- **Your camera angle is kept.** When the over-the-shoulder camera takes over
  again, it keeps the angle you had, as long as Miku is standing in the same
  spot and facing the same way. After the Camera Obscura, or if a script
  moves her, it starts again behind her.

## Build

- CI packages are now Debug builds too.

## Installing

1. Download the zip for your platform and your game's region: **US** for the
   NTSC-U (USA) disc, **EU** for the PAL (EU) disc. NTSC-J discs are not
   supported.
2. Extract it, then copy `IMG_HD.BIN` and `IMG_BD.BIN` from your disc next to
   the `MikuPan` executable.
3. In game, open **Settings › Game › Camera** and set **Camera View** to
   **Over the Shoulder**.

These packages are Debug builds, as the README recommends: the Release
configuration has known issues.
