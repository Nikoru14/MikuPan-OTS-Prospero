## New

- **Scare Camera setting** in **Settings › Game › Camera**. The game frames
  its scares and staged moments with scripted camera angles; choose what
  the over-the-shoulder camera does when one plays:
  - **Guided** (default): stay over the shoulder. If what the shot frames is
    off screen, turn towards it once, in about half a second. Moving the
    right stick or mouse takes over immediately.
  - **Original:** cut to the game's own shot, in play and in events, then
    come back afterwards.
  - **Off:** ignore scripted angles.
- **Steady movement across camera cuts.** If the view cuts to a scripted
  shot or back while you hold the stick, Miku keeps walking the same way
  until you let go; then the stick follows the camera on screen.
- Translations for the new option.

## Installing

1. Download the zip for your platform and your game's region: **US** for the
   NTSC-U (USA) disc, **EU** for the PAL (EU) disc. NTSC-J discs are not
   supported.
2. Extract it, then copy `IMG_HD.BIN` and `IMG_BD.BIN` from your disc next to
   the `MikuPan` executable.
3. In game, open **Settings › Game › Camera** and set **Camera View** to
   **Over the Shoulder**.

These packages are Debug builds, as the README recommends: the Release
configuration has known issues.
